Terms of use

Website concept · Last updated October 6, 2026

These terms describe this local HealthNavGZ website and training concept. They are not a commercial service agreement.

Use of this website

You may explore the site and its sample training scenarios for informational purposes. The scenario builder displays preset content in your browser and does not create or retain records.

Training and navigation scope

HealthNavGZ describes training for digital health navigators who help people use digital health tools and access virtual care. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, triage, or clinical advice. Navigators should follow their organization’s policies, professional scope, privacy requirements, and escalation procedures.

AI and automation statements

The website presents a product concept for AI-assisted operational workflows. No live AI model, patient system, device provisioning service, or organization platform is connected. Any checklists are illustrative training examples and should be adapted and reviewed by qualified program leaders before use.

Privacy and sensitive information

Do not submit patient names, diagnoses, or identifying health information through this website. The sample demo does not need such information.

Availability and production use

This local prototype is provided for demonstration and may change or become unavailable. Before operating a live service, the organization should publish reviewed terms that match actual functionality, applicable laws, training design, and data practices.
