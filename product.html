ONE CONNECTED OPERATING MODEL

Training and operations,
working together.

Bring learning paths, referral workflows, and program improvement into one practical approach. AI-enabled coordination can reduce administrative friction while staff retain control and human connection.

01 / PREPARE

Develop navigator skills

Structure foundational learning in digital inclusion, communication, privacy awareness, and virtual visit support.

02 / COORDINATE

Organize operational workflows

Clarify referrals, follow-up steps, escalation routes, and team roles across a navigator program.

03 / LEARN

Improve the program

Use appropriate, de-identified program signals and feedback to find workflow gaps and training needs.

HUMAN-CENTERED BY DESIGN

Technology supports.
People make care personal.

Navigators help people build confidence and connect to resources. The platform concept supports that work; it does not make clinical decisions or replace qualified care teams.

Clear boundaries

Keep navigation, clinical care, and technical support roles distinct.

Accessible coaching

Use plain language, teach-back, and respect each person’s choices.

Thoughtful automation

Route and organize operational work while keeping sensitive steps reviewable.
