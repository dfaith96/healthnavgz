A PEOPLE-FIRST WORKFLOW

Guide the person.
Strengthen the system.

Operational automation helps organizations coordinate training and support. Navigators bring the empathy, context, and judgment that technology cannot replace.

01

Understand the need

Start with the person’s goal, preferred language, access needs, and comfort level.

02

Practice together

Use a clear, teach-back approach to practice the digital task without taking control away.

03

Connect the support

Coordinate devices, connectivity, accessibility resources, or provider follow-up as appropriate.

04

Learn and improve

Capture de-identified program trends to strengthen training and remove access barriers.
