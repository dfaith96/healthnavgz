Privacy policy

Website concept · Last updated October 6, 2026

This is a local training-platform concept, not a deployed health service. No patient information should be entered into its sample workflow.

Information entered in the demo

The interactive training sandbox uses preset examples in your browser. It does not ask for, transmit, or store patient details, diagnoses, or other personal health information. Avoid entering sensitive information into any demo fields.

Contact links

The contact link opens a draft email in your email application. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. The displayed recipient, hello@healthnavgz.com, is the intended contact address; verify that the inbox is active before public use.

Technical information

This prototype has no account system, analytics, advertising trackers, or backend. The page may request fonts from Google Fonts when online; system fonts are used if that request is unavailable.

Future services

If HealthNavGZ later adds accounts, learning records, organization dashboards, patient workflows, or third-party services, the operator should update this policy to explain what information is collected, why it is used, how long it is kept, who can access it, and how users can exercise their rights.

Contact

Use the organization contact link to prepare a message. Replace the placeholder address before launch.

This policy is illustrative and should be reviewed and adapted to the actual service and applicable privacy requirements before deployment.
